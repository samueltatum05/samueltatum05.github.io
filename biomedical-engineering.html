Biomedical Engineering

The Big Picture

Applied engineering work aimed at improving healthcare and accessibility, spanning research to senior capstone design. The research project is a MATLAB image-analysis pipeline that reads raw confocal microscopy data and quantifies where the fluorescent compound localizes within cells and tissue. The capstone project is an affordable mechanical wheelchair lift for pickup truck beds, developed in partnership with Stride SCI, to help wheelchair users stow their chairs and travel with greater independence.
