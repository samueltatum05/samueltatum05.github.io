Data Center Rack Liquid Cooling System

Summary

This project targeted the urgent sustainability and thermal management challenges facing next-generation, high-density data centers. To efficiently reject 288 kW of waste heat from an 18U liquid-cooled server rack, we analyzed the fluid dynamic trade-offs between heat exchanger channel geometry and total system hydraulic impedance. By prioritizing energy efficiency across the entire fluid loop, we engineered a custom Coolant Distribution Unit (CDU) integrating a high-effectiveness counter-flow heat exchanger with a centrifugal pump calibrated to its peak efficiency operating point. This architecture eliminates thermal bottlenecks by reliably cooling returning rack water from 44°C down to 20°C while overcoming complete system head loss on just 0.25 HP. Ultimately, the design demonstrates a scalable, energy-optimized liquid cooling framework critical for reducing facility energy overhead and enabling sustainable, high-performance computing infrastructure worldwide.
