Cold Plate Liquid Cooling System

Summary

This project tackled the critical thermal management challenges of high-density computer hardware. To cool an eight-processor server rack, we analyzed the thermal and hydraulic trade-offs between parallel and pin-fin cold plates. By prioritizing overall system efficiency, we engineered a parallel flow loop with custom manifolds rather than a traditional series layout. This design choice eliminated cascading temperature gradients (where downstream chips overheat from upstream exhaust) and drastically reduced total system pressure drop. Ultimately, the architecture safely maintains sensitive components below their 60°C thermal limits while minimizing the required pump power and demonstrating a scalable approach to enabling faster and more energy-efficient data centers.
