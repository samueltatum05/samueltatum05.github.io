Deep Space Probe Power System

Summary

Operating in the vacuum of space requires strict reliance on radiative heat transfer and highly robust power generation cycles. This project modeled a deep space power system, exploring thermodynamic cycles for electricity generation (utilizing radioisotope or solar heat sources) while safely rejecting waste heat to deep space via optimized radiator panels.
