Thermal Energy Storage

The Big Picture

This study investigated the design and parametric optimization of a thermal energy storage system (TES), critical for decoupling energy collection (such as solar thermal) from energy dispatch. The focus was on selecting appropriate storage mediums, determining necessary volume and insulation parameters, and modeling transient charge and discharge rates.
