Wheelchair Truck Lift

Summary

Team capstone project for Villanova Mechanical Engineering, developed in partnership with Stride SCI, a neurorehabilitation gym and training facility for individuals living with paralysis. Getting into and out of a vehicle and stowing a wheelchair without damaging it is a physically demanding part of daily life for many wheelchair users, so the team is designing an affordable and functional lift that mounts in the bed of a pickup truck and transfers a manual wheelchair between the ground beside the driver’s door and the truck bed. The goal is to reduce the physical effort of transport while promoting independence for the people who use it.

The design works like a small crane with a winch and is broken into two subsystems: the mount, a rack that attaches to the top of the truck bed’s side rails, and the lift, a rotating crane arm with an integrated winch and a carabiner that secures the wheelchair. Rather than custom-manufacturing every component, the team is building around pre-built products with minor customization to keep cost and build time down and make the design easy for others to replicate. Key requirements include installation in under 60 minutes with no permanent modifications to the truck, and no damage to the truck or wheelchair during operation.

The project is currently in the detailed design phase, with the team aiming to present a working prototype on the university truck by the end of April.
