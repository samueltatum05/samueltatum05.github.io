Competition Robotics

Evaluated thermodynamic and hydraulic trade-offs—balancing heat transfer effectiveness against pressure drop and pump power demands—while physically fabricating and testing functional heat exchangers (including shell-and-tube configurations) to empirically validate theoretical models.
