Thermal Fluid Systems Design

The Big Picture

Designed, modeled, and parametrically optimized thermal-fluid systems across diverse applications ranging from solar thermal energy storage to data center GPU server rack cooling loops. Evaluated thermodynamic and hydraulic trade-offs (balancing heat transfer effectiveness against pressure drop and pump power demands) while physically fabricating and testing a functional heat exchanger and cold plate to empirically validate theoretical models.
