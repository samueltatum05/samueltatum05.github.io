Data Center Research

Summary

As a Research Assistant on the Carrier Thermal Fluids Research Project at Villanova University, I'm developing component-level models of datacenter cooling devices as a contractor for Carrier. Applying heat transfer and fluid mechanics principles, these models are built to predict how datacenter cooling devices will perform.

Approach & Collaboration

The work centers on modeling cooling devices at the component level, grounding each prediction in the underlying heat transfer and fluid mechanics. I collaborate with faculty and industry engineers to translate the modeling results into design insights for next-generation cooling systems, connecting analytical work directly to the engineering decisions that shape future designs.

Confidentiality

This project is conducted under contract with Carrier and is protected by a non-disclosure agreement, so specific methods, models, and results cannot be shared publicly. It is included here to reflect the ongoing research I am contributing to alongside my other thermal fluids work.
