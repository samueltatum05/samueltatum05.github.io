Mini Heat Exchanger

Summary

Transitioning from theoretical calculations to physical hardware, this project involved the end-to-end fabrication of a functional mini heat exchanger. To convert waste thermal energy from hot water into usable warm airflow, we analyzed the material and heat-transfer trade-offs across a crossflow heat exchanger, balancing thermal conductivity, structural strength, and insulation performance within a strict cubic-foot footprint. It required aluminum plate machining, precision tubing integration, and physical thermal testing to empirically validate the mathematical heat transfer models developed in the earlier stages.
