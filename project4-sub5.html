Mini Heat Exchanger

The Big Picture

Transitioning from theoretical calculations to physical hardware, this project involved the end-to-end fabrication of a functional mini heat exchanger and cold plate. It required aluminum plate machining, precision tubing integration, and physical thermal testing to empirically validate the mathematical heat transfer models developed in the earlier stages.
