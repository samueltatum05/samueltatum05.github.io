Peru Project: Sustainable Energy Engineering

Summary

As a volunteer with the WindAid Institute in Trujillo, Peru (September–December 2024), I helped build and deploy a small-scale wind energy system from raw materials to a working installation. The fabrication work included manufacturing fiberglass composite wind turbine blades and welding a structural steel tower, along with building and installing a wooden electrical enclosure that integrates a DC-DC buck converter to regulate the turbine’s 12V output down to 5V, producing usable electricity for lightbulbs and phone chargers. Once a 15-ft wind turbine was installed in the Andes, I collected and analyzed anemometer data to assess the site’s wind performance.
